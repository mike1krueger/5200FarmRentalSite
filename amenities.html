Barn

A timber frame barn with two stalls, finished with rubber matting, and room to expand to four stalls. Your horses are kept under cover and close to the ring and field, so everything you need each day is within a short walk.

A tack room keeps saddles and gear organized, and there is ample storage throughout, including a loft for hay and supplies.

Field

An open field of about 6 acres, enclosed by traditional three-board oak fencing, with an automatic horse waterer.

The pasture is well established, with thick, healthy grass that holds up to grazing and riding. Use it for turnout, conditioning work or simply extra space around the barn and ring.

Ring

A full-size dressage ring, 197 by 66 feet (60 by 20 meters), with a crushed gravel surface, for your exclusive use.

Schedule your own lessons, schooling and training without waiting for a turn or sharing the space with other riders. The ring sits beside the barn and field, so tacking up, riding and cooling out all happen within a few steps.
