Barn

A covered barn with two stalls, rubber matting in the stalls, and an upstairs. The barn can be upgraded to four stalls. Your horses are kept under cover and close to the ring and field, so everything you need each day is within a short walk. The barn is yours alone for the length of the rental, with no other tenants on site.

Field

An open field with room to ride, hack and park. The field has an automatic horse waterer. Use it for conditioning work, turnout or simply extra space around the barn and ring. Like the rest of the property, the field is exclusively yours for the month.

Ring

A riding ring reserved for your use throughout the month. The ring is Olympic sized, with a crushed gravel surface. Schedule your own lessons, schooling and training without waiting for a turn or sharing the space with other riders. The ring sits beside the barn and field, which keeps tacking up, riding and cooling out within one stretch of the property.
